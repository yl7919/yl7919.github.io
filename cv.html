Research

Job Market Paper

Characteristic Libraries and Portfolio Decisions

Working paper, September 2026

Develops an empirical framework that judges updates to the characteristic library of a machine-learning portfolio by their investment consequences, using controls that add no information (duplicated or re-weighted themes) to separate re-weighting of existing signals from new information.

Working Papers

Characteristic-Space Metrics in Factor Models: Identification and Inference

SSRN preprint, September 2026. DOI: 10.2139/ssrn.7445120

Shows that the inner product placed on characteristic space determines how models with identical fit split expected returns among latent factors, observed factors and an intercept, and develops coordinate-free inference when this metric is estimated from data.

A Geometric Framework for Identification in Characteristic-Based Factor Models

SSRN preprint, March 2026. DOI: 10.2139/ssrn.7013178

Measures characteristics with an empirical metric derived from their long-run cross-sectional distribution, states identification and no-arbitrage restrictions as geometric properties, and proposes No-Arbitrage IPCA (NA-IPCA), which nests IPCA and admits rotation-invariant inference.

Characteristic Geometry and Portfolio Choice

Working paper, September 2026

Traces why factor models with almost identical return forecasts hold different portfolios to a fixed ridge adjustment to factor variance, which penalises exposures differently across loading representations, and proposes comparing models under a common risk adjustment.

Interpreting Estimated Pricing Errors: Evidence from Characteristic-Based Return Forecasts

Working paper, September 2026

Separates changes in fitted expected returns from changes in factor loading spaces to ask what a smaller estimated pricing error tells a model user and whether pricing-error diagnostics help in choosing or adjusting a return forecast.

PhD Thesis

No-Arbitrage IPCA: A Framework for Cross-Sectional Asset Pricing

Imperial College London; PhD conferred August 2026

Develops an empirical characteristic metric and a nested sequence of models (IPCA, QZ-IPCA and NA-IPCA) that separates latent, observable and residual pricing components, with identification results, asymptotic theory and an estimation algorithm.
